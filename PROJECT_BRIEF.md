# Project Brief — After Work Social Presence Agent

## The book

*After Work: Finding Meaning, Identity, and Purpose When the World No Longer
Needs Your Labor*. Narrative nonfiction / self-help by John Mansoor, PsyD.

Core thesis: Work has been humanity's psychological infrastructure for
millennia — providing structure, competence, belonging, identity, and meaning.
AI is dismantling that infrastructure faster than anyone is prepared for. The
interesting question is not which jobs survive. It is what work was doing for us
that we did not know about, and what to build instead. The book is framed for a
30-50 year horizon, not the current news cycle.

Comparable titles: *Four Thousand Weeks* (Burkeman), *From Strength to Strength*
(Brooks).

## The problem this agent solves

John needs an author platform to attract literary agents and, eventually,
readers. He has no current public presence, no time to run content marketing,
and no marketing background. His credibility is clinical, not promotional. The
agent turns his clinical insight into a consistent, voice-accurate social
presence without requiring him to become a content marketer.

## Target platforms

- **Substack** — long-form home base. Notes are the primary discovery engine.
  One long-form essay per week; daily Notes.
- **LinkedIn** — professional amplifier. Interest-graph algorithm means a near-
  empty account can still get reach. Document/carousel posts and comment
  engagement are the growth levers. 3-4 posts per week.

## Content pillars (all content maps to one)

1. **Clinical Window** — composite, anonymized patient stories.
2. **The Thesis** — the book's core argument from different angles.
3. **Personal** — John's own relationship with AI; vulnerability; writing the book.
4. **Applied Philosophy** — Frankl, Aristotle, Stoics, Ubuntu, made accessible.
5. **Current Events (psychological lens)** — react to AI news as a clinician.

## Voice (summary; full version in STYLE_GUIDE.md)

Direct, clinically precise, dry humor. Vulnerable without self-pity. Opens on a
specific moment or body in a room. Short sentences when a point lands. Names the
feeling before the theory. Ends unresolved, not with a bow. Never preachy, never
alarmist, never thought-leader smooth. Avoids a long list of AI-tell words and
constructions (negative parallelisms, rule-of-three padding, puffery, em-dash
overuse, the word "quietly," and more).

## Future expansion (do not build yet)

John also runs a portable TMS (transcranial magnetic stimulation) service and a
business/executive consulting practice. These brands fold into the platform
later. For now everything centers on the book and the psychology + AI space.
When John says it is time, design a brand architecture first; do not just start
mixing in TMS and consulting content.
